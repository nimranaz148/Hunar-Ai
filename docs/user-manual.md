## What is Hunar?

Hunar v2 is an AI-powered platform specifically built for informal women micro-entrepreneurs in Pakistan who run home-based businesses in apparel, home baking, crafts, and artisan goods.

### What Hunar Does

Hunar helps you:
- **Validate Your Business Idea**: Check if your product can succeed in your local market
- **Calculate Unit Economics**: Understand your costs, pricing, and profit margins
- **Create a Launch Plan**: Get a step-by-step 30-day roadmap to start your business
- **Generate Social Content**: Build a complete content calendar with daily post ideas
- **Track Your Performance**: Log daily metrics (views, likes, inquiries, orders) and see visual analytics
- **Connect with Mentors**: Book verified mentors for 1-on-1 guidance and support
- **Get Weekly Coaching**: Receive performance reviews and strategic recommendations

### What Makes Hunar Different

1. **Roman Urdu Conversation**: Talk to Hunar naturally in Roman Urdu — no complex English forms
2. **True AI Agents**: Multiple specialized AI agents work together autonomously
3. **Human Approval Gates**: You must approve every major plan before Hunar proceeds
4. **Complete Lifecycle**: From idea to daily execution, everything in one place
5. **Verified Mentors**: All mentors are verified professionals with real expertise
6. **Enterprise Governance**: Super Admin oversight ensures quality and safety

## For Entrepreneurs

### Step 1: Onboarding — Tell Hunar Your Idea

1. Click **"Onboarding"** in the sidebar
2. You'll see a chat interface
3. Describe your business idea in Roman Urdu:
   ```
   Example: "Assalamu alaikum! Main apna home-based pret brand start
   karna chahti hun. Budget 50,000 PKR hai. Lahore mein rehti hun."
   ```
4. Hunar's **Intake Agent** will ask clarifying questions:
   - What products will you make?
   - Who are your customers?
   - What's your budget?
   - Where are you located?
5. The **Validator Agent** will research your market:
   - Competitor analysis
   - Market gap identification
   - Underserved opportunity assessment

**What You'll Get:**
- A detailed **Validation Report** with confidence score
- Market research and competitor landscape
- Next steps recommendation

---

### Step 2: Calculate Unit Economics

After validation, Hunar's **Pricing Agent** will:
1. Ask about your **costs**:
   - Raw materials (per unit)
   - Labor (your time per piece)
   - Overhead (electricity, packaging, shipping)
2. Research market pricing for similar products
3. Calculate your recommended **selling price**
4. Show **margin breakdown** (cost vs. profit)

**Navigate to Pricing:**
- Click **"Pricing"** in sidebar
- View detailed cost breakdown table
- See profit margin percentage
- Understand recommended pricing

**Example:**
```
Product: Pret Kameez
Material Cost: PKR 800
Labor Cost: PKR 300
Overhead: PKR 100
Total Cost: PKR 1,200
Recommended Price: PKR 1,800
Profit Margin: PKR 600 (33%)
```

---

### Step 3: Review Your Launch Plan (GATE 1)

Hunar's **Launch Planner Agent** creates a 30-day roadmap with actionable tasks.

**Navigate to Plan:**
1. Click **"Plan"** in sidebar
2. Review the **Launch Plan Overview**:
   - Business summary
   - Target customers
   - Pricing strategy
   - Marketing channels
3. See your **30-Day Task List**:
   - Week 1: Sample creation, photography
   - Week 2: Social media setup
   - Week 3: First posts, engagement
   - Week 4: Launch campaign, first sales

**IMPORTANT — Human Approval Gate 1:**
- You must **approve** or **reject** the plan
- If approved, Hunar proceeds to content generation
- If rejected, provide feedback for revision

**How to Approve:**
1. Read the full plan carefully
2. Click **"Approve Plan"** button
3. Confirm approval in dialog

**How to Reject:**
1. Click **"Reject Plan"** button
2. Enter your feedback/concerns
3. Hunar will revise and present a new plan

---

### Step 4: Content Calendar (GATE 2)

After you approve the plan, Hunar's **Content Strategist Agent** creates:
- **30-day content calendar**
- Daily post ideas with captions (Roman Urdu)
- Product showcase posts
- Behind-the-scenes content
- Customer testimonials
- Educational tips

**Navigate to Content:**
1. Click **"Content"** in sidebar
2. View **Calendar View**: See all 30 days at a glance
3. View **Today View**: Focus on today's post
4. View **Outline**: See content pillars and themes

**IMPORTANT — Human Approval Gate 2:**
- You must approve the **Content Outline** before daily posts unlock
- Review the 4-week themes and pillar topics
- Approve or request revisions

**How to Use Content:**
1. Each day, check **"Today View"**
2. See suggested post caption (Roman Urdu)
3. Mark as **"Posted"** when you publish
4. Mark as **"Skipped"** if you skip the day
5. Edit caption if needed before posting

**Example Daily Post:**
```
Day 3: Product Showcase
Caption: "Meri nayi pret collection ready hai! Hand-embroidered lawn
suits in pastel colors. Price PKR 1,800. DM for orders!"
Image: Photo of your product
```

### Step 5: Log Daily Metrics

Track your business performance by logging daily metrics.

**Navigate to Metrics:**
1. Click **"Metrics"** in sidebar
2. Click **"Log Today's Metrics"** button
3. Enter numbers for:
   - **Content Views**: How many people saw your post?
   - **Likes/Hearts**: How many likes/reactions?
   - **Reach**: Total people reached (if available)
   - **Inquiries**: How many DMs/calls asking about product?
   - **Orders**: How many actual orders received?
   - **Revenue (PKR)**: Total sales amount today
4. Click **"Save Metrics"**

**View Analytics:**
- **Charts**: See line graphs for all metrics over time
- **Totals**: View cumulative numbers
- **Channel Breakdown**: See performance by channel (Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp)
- **Top Posts**: See which content performed best

**Why This Matters:**
- Tracks your growth week-over-week
- Helps identify what content works
- Triggers weekly coach reviews

---

### Step 6: Weekly Coach Review (GATE 3)

Every 7 days, Hunar's **Weekly Coach Agent** analyzes your performance and proposes:
- What's working well
- What needs improvement
- Recommended content adjustments
- Suggested next steps

**Navigate to Proposals:**
1. Click **"Proposals"** in sidebar
2. See list of weekly reviews
3. Click a proposal to view details

**Review Contents:**
- **Diagnosis**: Analysis of your metrics (views, engagement, conversions)
- **Strengths**: What you're doing right
- **Weaknesses**: Where you're struggling
- **Proposed Adjustments**: Specific changes to your content calendar
- **Next Steps**: Action items for next week

**IMPORTANT — Human Approval Gate 3:**
- You must **approve** or **reject** coach recommendations
- If approved, Hunar updates your content calendar
- If rejected, no changes are made

**How to Approve:**
1. Read the diagnosis and recommendations
2. Click **"Approve Proposal"**
3. Confirm — your content calendar will update

**How to Reject:**
1. Click **"Reject Proposal"**
2. Enter why you disagree
3. Calendar stays unchanged

### Step 7: Book a Mentor

When you need 1-on-1 human guidance, book a verified mentor.

**Navigate to Mentors:**
1. Click **"Mentors"** in sidebar
2. Browse available mentors
3. Filter by:
   - Category (Apparel, Food, Crafts, Marketing, Finance)
   - Location (Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad, etc.)
   - Availability

**View Mentor Profile:**
- Bio and expertise
- Services offered (e.g., "Instagram Marketing Setup — PKR 1,000/hour")
- Availability calendar (green = available, gray = booked)
- Reviews and ratings

**Book a Session:**
1. Click **"Book Session"** on mentor profile
2. Select a service
3. Choose an available time slot
4. Review booking details
5. Click **"Confirm Booking"**
6. **Payment**: Amount is held in escrow (not paid yet)

**Session Flow:**
1. **Booked**: Mentor confirms, you receive notification
2. **Confirmed**: Session scheduled
3. **Completed**: After session, mentor marks complete
4. **Review**: You leave rating and review
5. **Paid**: After positive review, mentor receives payment from escrow

**How to Join Session:**
- Hunar sends reminder 1 day before
- Mentor will contact you via phone/video link
- Join at scheduled time

### Stalled Businesses Intervention

**What is "Stalled"?**
A business is stalled if:
- No metrics logged in 7+ days
- No content posted in 7+ days
- No plan approval action taken

**View Stalled List:**
1. Click **"Stalled"** tab in Cohort Dashboard
2. See list of inactive entrepreneurs:
   - Name, business
   - Days since last activity
   - Reason (e.g., "No metrics logged in 10 days")
   - Current stage

**Nudge a Stalled Business:**
1. Click on stalled entrepreneur
2. Click **"Send Nudge"**
3. System sends in-app notification + email:
   - "We notice you haven't logged metrics in 10 days. Need help? Book a mentor or reach out!"
4. Entrepreneur receives notification immediately

**Why This Matters:**
- Early intervention prevents dropout
- Shows entrepreneurs they're supported
- Improves cohort completion rates

## Common Questions

### For Entrepreneurs

**Q: What language can I use?**
A: Hunar supports **Roman Urdu** (Urdu written in English letters). You can also use English if comfortable, but Roman Urdu is recommended for best experience.

**Q: Do I need AI API keys?**
A: No. Entrepreneurs don't need any API keys. Everything is handled by the platform.

**Q: What if I skip a day of posting?**
A: Mark the content item as "Skipped". It's okay to skip occasionally, but consistency helps your metrics and weekly reviews will suggest improvements.

**Q: Can I edit the AI-generated content?**
A: Yes! Hunar's content is a suggestion. You can edit captions before posting to match your voice.

**Q: How do mentor payments work?**
A: When you book a mentor, the payment is held in escrow. After the session completes and you leave a review, the mentor receives the payment. This protects both parties.

**Q: What if I don't get my first order in 30 days?**
A: The weekly coach will analyze your metrics and suggest adjustments. You can also book a marketing mentor for personalized help.

**Q: Can I change my business idea mid-way?**
A: Yes, but you'll need to restart onboarding to generate a new validation report, pricing, and plan.

---

### For Mentors

**Q: How do I get paid?**
A: Payments are processed monthly. After sessions complete and entrepreneurs leave reviews, earnings move to your available balance. Payouts happen on the 1st of each month.

**Q: What if an entrepreneur doesn't show up?**
A: You can mark the session as "No Show" after waiting 15 minutes. The entrepreneur is still charged, and you receive the payment.

**Q: Can I offer free sessions?**
A: Yes. Set service price to PKR 0 for free consultations or pro-bono work.

**Q: How do I handle conflicts or issues with a mentee?**
A: Contact Super Admin via support. They can mediate disputes and review session notes.

**Q: What categories can I specialize in?**
A: Apparel & Fashion, Food & Baking, Crafts & Artisan Goods, Marketing & Social Media, Finance & Pricing, Operations & Logistics.

---

### For Super Admins

**Q: How often should I check stalled businesses?**
A: Daily review is recommended. Send nudges to businesses inactive >7 days.

**Q: What's a good completion rate?**
A: Target 30-40% of entrepreneurs reaching "First Order" within 60 days. Rates vary by cohort quality and support.

**Q: Should I disable features often?**
A: Only for emergencies, beta testing, or specific cohort experiments. Most flags should stay enabled for consistent user experience.

**Q: How do I create a new cohort?**
A: In User Management, create a cohort program (e.g., "Karachi Batch 2, Spring 2026"). Then assign new entrepreneurs to that cohort.

**Q: Can I export data?**
A: Currently manual export via database access. Future versions will include CSV/Excel export from dashboard.

## Troubleshooting

### Login Issues

**Problem:** "Invalid email or password"
- **Solution:** Check spelling, ensure Caps Lock is off. Use "Forgot Password" to reset.

**Problem:** "Account not found"
- **Solution:** Contact Super Admin — your account may not be created yet.

---

### Agent/Onboarding Issues

**Problem:** "Agent not responding" or loading forever
- **Solution:** Refresh the page. If persists, contact support — AI service may be down.

**Problem:** "Validation failed" or "Market research incomplete"
- **Solution:** Provide more details about your business, location, and target customers. Restart onboarding if needed.

---

### Content Issues

**Problem:** "Content calendar is empty"
- **Solution:** You must approve Gate 2 (Content Outline) first. Go to Content → Outline → Approve.

**Problem:** "Cannot edit content item"
- **Solution:** Once marked "Posted", items are locked. Contact admin to unlock if needed.

---

### Mentor Booking Issues

**Problem:** "No available slots" for mentor
- **Solution:** Mentor may be fully booked this week. Try another mentor or check back later.

**Problem:** "Payment failed"
- **Solution:** Ensure payment method is valid. Contact support for billing issues.

---

### Metrics Issues

**Problem:** "Cannot log metrics for today"
- **Solution:** You can only log each day once. If you need to update, edit the existing entry.

**Problem:** "Charts not updating"
- **Solution:** Charts update after you log metrics. If you just logged, refresh the page.

---

### General Issues

**Problem:** Page not loading or 404 error
- **Solution:** Check your internet connection. Try refreshing. If persists, server may be down — contact support.

**Problem:** Notifications not showing
- **Solution:** Check browser permissions for notifications. Enable in settings.

## Glossary

**Agent**: An AI program that performs specialized tasks (e.g., Intake Agent, Pricing Agent).

**Approval Gate**: A checkpoint where you must manually approve before Hunar proceeds (Gates 1, 2, 3).

**Cohort**: A group of entrepreneurs onboarded together (e.g., "Lahore Batch 1").

**Content Calendar**: Your 30-day plan of daily social media posts.

**Escrow**: A secure holding of payment until session completes and review is left.

**Feature Flag**: A system switch to enable/disable features.

**Human-in-the-Loop**: Hunar never acts without your explicit approval.

**Launch Plan**: Your 30-day roadmap of tasks to start your business.

**Metrics**: Daily numbers you log (views, likes, inquiries, orders, revenue).

**Orchestrator**: The main AI agent that coordinates all other agents.

**Pricing Agent**: AI agent that calculates costs, pricing, and margins.

**Roman Urdu**: Urdu language written using English alphabet (e.g., "Main apna business start karna chahti hun").

**Stalled Business**: An entrepreneur who hasn't been active in 7+ days.

**Super Admin**: Platform administrator who oversees cohorts and system health.

**Unit Economics**: Cost per product vs. selling price (understanding your profit).

**Validation Report**: Market research showing if your business idea can succeed.

**Weekly Coach**: AI agent that reviews your performance every 7 days.
